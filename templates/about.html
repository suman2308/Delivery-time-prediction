About Us

We build delivery predictions you can rely on

CourierAI exists to close the gap between what couriers promise and what they can actually deliver — with machine learning trained on real shipment history.

Our story

Every day, thousands of shipments move between Indian cities — by surface, by express courier, and by air. Yet most delivery timelines were still set with rules of thumb and gut feeling.

We set out to change that. Starting from a dataset of nearly 50,000 real courier records, we trained a gradient-boosting model that learns how origin, destination, mode, and parcel characteristics shape delivery duration — and packaged it into a platform any operations team can use.

What we believe

  • Estimates should be evidence-based. Predictions come from data, not intuition.
  • Models should be explainable. Every result carries its version and its inputs.
  • Speed matters. Insights are only useful if they arrive before the shipment moves.
